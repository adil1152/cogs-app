The user says Income and Cost service quantities usually match (for example Catering, Laundry, Additional services and Snacks), and wants per-service linking in project settings.

**Why:** Avoid duplicate quantity entry without preventing exceptional Cost quantities.

**How to apply:** Follow the linked Income quantity until the user manually edits Cost quantity. Never rewrite saved quantities on reopen, and never copy Income amounts or rates. A defaulted quantity without a Cost amount is unfinished, not an automatic zero transaction.
