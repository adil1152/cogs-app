---
name: MIS resume draft expectations
description: User-confirmed meaning of resuming a partially completed bulk entry.
---

Resuming a draft means reopening the complete project/period entry form,
including unfinished services, rather than showing only persisted lines.
Continue using the same bulk transaction and preserve previously saved values.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed that saving halfway through entry must
allow them to resume from that stage without losing access to unentered services.

**How to apply:** Treat unfinished services as blank form rows, not automatic
zero-value transactions. Preserve saved snapshots even when a service is later
disabled. Keep edit permissions and approval workflows intact.
